🎮 Game Overview

Sky Orb Collector is a gaze-based mobile VR game where you collect glowing orbs by looking at them.

🕹 Controls

ActionHow to do it
Aim at an orbMove your mouse / head
Collect an orbHold gaze for ~1.5 seconds

🎯 How to Play

  • Move your mouse (or head in VR) to aim the glowing ring cursor at orbs
  • Keep the cursor focused on an orb for about 1.5 seconds — the ring fills and collects it automatically
  • Cyan/blue orbs: +10 points
  • Red orbs: −5 points
  • Each round lasts 60 seconds — try to score as high as possible before time runs out!

🏆 Scoring

  • Green orb: +10
  • Red orb: −5
