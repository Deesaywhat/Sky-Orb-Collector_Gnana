🎮 Game Overview

Sky Orb Collector is a gaze-based mobile VR game built with A-Frame. The player stands in the centre of a floating sky world and collects glowing orbs by simply looking at them. No hands, no controller: only your gaze.

Collect as many blue orbs (+10 points) as possible before the 60-second timer runs out. In Level 2, beware red "bad orbs" (-5 points) that deduct points!

📦 Installation & Setup

No build tools or server required. The game runs in any modern browser.

  1. 1. Download the file: obtain index.html
  2. 2. Open in browser: double-click or use Live Server
  3. 3. Allow motion access on mobile devices
  4. 4. Click VR icon and insert into Cardboard

📱💻🥽 Where It Runs

  • Computer (Laptop/Desktop): Open the file in a browser and use your mouse to look around and collect orbs
  • Phone: Open in browser and move your phone to aim using motion sensors
  • VR Mode: Tap the VR icon, place your phone in a Cardboard headset, and use head movement to play

🎯 How to Play

  • Aim at orbs using your mouse or head
  • Look at an orb for 1.5 seconds to collect it
  • Blue orbs: +10
  • Red orbs: −5
  • You have 60 seconds to score as high as possible

🏆 Scoring

  • Blue orb = +10
  • Red orb = −5
